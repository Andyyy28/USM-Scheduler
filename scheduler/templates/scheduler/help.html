Core procedure

Seven controlled steps

  1. 1

    Prepare an academic term

    Confirm academic year, semester, Kabacan campus, teaching days, atom length, and semester dates. Use a new dataset revision for every material change.

  2. 2

    Prepare the workbook

    Enter catalog records, active sections, fixed instructors, room capabilities and authorizations, time atoms, availability, offerings, and meeting requirements. Do not add room capacity or identifiable student data to this v1 study.

  3. 3

    Preview and commit

    Preview validates references and rules without changing the semester dataset. Correct every error, preview again, then commit the entire clean batch atomically.

  4. 4

    Preflight and freeze input

    Select an approved objective profile. Preflight removes illegal room-time candidates and rejects missing availability, unusable locks, or meetings with no legal placement. Store the immutable snapshot and its hash.

  5. 5

    Generate and compare

    Run CP-SAT and the Genetic Algorithm with the same snapshot, objective, hardware allocation, and time limit. Feasibility comes first; compare quality only among independently validated schedules.

  6. 6

    Review and revise

    College reviewers inspect their unit, comment, and endorse. The central scheduler resolves concerns, locks confirmed meetings when needed, and creates a child version for regeneration.

  7. 7

    Approve and export

    Only a zero-hard-violation version can proceed. Central approval makes the version immutable; export the approved schedule and its reproducibility manifest for authorized distribution.

Responsibilities

Who does what

System administrator

Controls accounts, role assignments, configuration, backups, and platform maintenance. This role does not certify academic correctness.

Central scheduler

Owns term preparation, imports, snapshots, optimization runs, final conflict checks, approvals, locks, and authorized exports.

College reviewer

Views the complete timetable for context and comments or endorses only the assigned college scope.

Interpretation

Solver outcomes

Optimal
CP-SAT found a feasible schedule and proved no better objective exists for that model.
Feasible
A zero-hard-violation schedule was found; optimality was not proved.
Infeasible
Use only when CP-SAT proves the model has no solution. GA cannot make this claim.
No solution found
The time-limited search ended without a validated feasible result; this does not prove impossibility.
Invalid input
Preflight found a data or policy problem that must be corrected before a fair algorithm run.

Preview reports errors

Use the reported sheet, row, cell, and code. Resolve missing references first, then availability, authorization, capabilities, and locks.

A meeting has no candidate

Check its duration, break boundaries, fixed instructors, room capability, campus, availability, and every attached section's authorization rule.

A solver times out

Keep the result as a recorded timeout. Do not call it infeasible, silently retry it, or alter only one algorithm's budget.
