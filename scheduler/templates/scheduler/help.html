Prepare

Set up the semester and its data

  1. Open Academic Terms and confirm the correct semester.
  2. Download a workbook, enter the semester information, and open Prepare Data.
  3. Upload the workbook and choose Check workbook.
  4. Correct every listed error. When the check is clean, save the prepared data.
Why does the workbook need to be checked?

It prevents missing instructors, invalid room assignments, overlapping availability, and incomplete scheduling rules from reaching the scheduler.

2

Generate

Build a candidate timetable

  1. Open Generate Schedule.
  2. Choose the prepared semester and quality policy, then check the data.
  3. Choose the checked dataset and keep the recommended scheduling method.
  4. Select Generate timetable. You may leave the page while it runs.
  5. Open the result when its status changes from Running.

Tip: The advanced Research tools are not needed to generate a normal timetable.

Troubleshooting

Common problems

The workbook has errors

Start with missing references, then check availability, room permission, room capabilities, and locked meetings. Re-upload the corrected workbook.

A meeting has no available placement

Check its duration, instructor availability, room type, teaching-day boundaries, breaks, and college room authorization.

Generation ended without a schedule

This does not always mean a valid timetable is impossible. Review the result details, data check, and time limit before trying again.

I cannot see an action button

Your role or the schedule's current status may not allow that action. The central scheduler prepares and approves; college reviewers record assigned decisions.
